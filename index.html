The real problem with using ChatGPT for fitness

🔢

Numbers drift

ChatGPT reads your calorie targets from conversation history, not a database. When the chat is long, it miscalculates. Real example: "let's add 100 kcal, new goal is 2250" — the real target was 2300. FitCoach AI stores your macros as structured data. The number is always accurate.

📜

One chat becomes a mess

To correlate your workout plan with nutrition, everything has to live in one thread. Months in, you're scrolling through walls of text. Our founder literally copy-pasted his training plan and diet plan into Excel just to see them side by side.

🧱

Context window cliff

Eventually the chat fills up and you have to start over — losing months of history, re-establishing your baseline from scratch. FitCoach AI stores your data persistently across unlimited sessions.

📊

No dashboard — just a chat box

ChatGPT is a chat interface, not a fitness tool. FitCoach AI shows your workout schedule, daily macro targets (adjusted for activity), weekly progress, and goal trend — all in one place. No Excel required.

How FitCoach AI actually works

1

Tell FitCoach about yourself — once

Your stats, goals, activity schedule (like swimming on Saturdays), and dietary preferences. The AI builds a complete picture and keeps it forever.

2

Get your weekly plan

FitCoach designs your macro targets and workout schedule for the week — correlated together, adjusted for each day's activity. Higher activity day? More carbs. Rest day? Weekly check-in. Adaptive coaching.

Every week, FitCoach reviews your actual numbers against the plan. It adjusts targets, catches drift before it becomes a problem, and keeps your plan evolving as your body changes.
